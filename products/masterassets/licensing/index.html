MasterAssets Licensing

A plain-language summary of how MasterAssets licenses work.

1

One-time purchase

No subscription. Pay once, get a license key and a perpetual license to that major version.

2

Per major version

Your license covers one major version line (currently 1.0.0). A future 2.0.0 release needs a new license.

3

Per user

Each license is issued to one individual. Teams need one license per person using MasterAssets.

4

Indie or Commercial

Pricing is tiered by company revenue. Same product and features either way - see below.

1. What a license covers

Purchasing MasterAssets grants a single named user a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to install and use the major version they purchased (currently 1.0.0). Bug fixes and minor updates released within that major version (e.g. 1.1.0, 1.2.0) are included at no extra cost for as long as they're released.

2. Indie vs. Commercial pricing

MasterAssets is priced in two tiers based on the buyer's scale. Both tiers unlock the exact same product - there is no feature difference between them, only price.

Tier Price Who it's for
Indie £39.90 Individuals, freelancers, and studios/companies whose most recent full financial year's gross revenue was under £90,000.
Commercial £79.90 Studios/companies whose most recent full financial year's gross revenue was £90,000 or more.

The £90,000 line is set to match the UK VAT registration threshold - a fixed, externally defined figure rather than one we set arbitrarily. If your organization's home country uses a different standard for distinguishing small/early-stage businesses from larger ones, use your local equivalent as a guide.

Tier is self-certified at checkout - we don't audit financials before a sale. If it turns out a purchase was made at the Indie price by an organization that qualified as Commercial at the time of purchase, EspositoLabs may require payment of the price difference and/or revoke the license until the correct tier is purchased. If your revenue grows past the threshold after you've already bought a license, that license remains valid for the major version you bought - the tier only applies again when you purchase a license for a future major version.

3. New major versions require a new license

MasterAssets is licensed per major version, not for life. If EspositoLabs ships a new major version (for example, MasterAssets 2.0.0), your existing license does not carry over to it - a separate license is required to use the new major version. Existing customers may be offered a discounted upgrade price when a new major version launches, at EspositoLabs' discretion, but this isn't guaranteed and would be announced separately.

4. One license, one user

Licenses are issued per individual, not per machine and not per company. The same license key may be used across a licensed user's own devices (e.g. a home and work machine), but it is not intended for simultaneous use by, or sharing between, multiple people. If several people at an organization need to use MasterAssets, each of them needs their own license.

5. What teams and companies should know

  • Seat-based, not floating. There's no network or floating license pool - license count should match the number of individuals actively using the software.
  • Buying for a team. Purchase one license per artist/user who will run MasterAssets. For larger studios needing several seats at once, email us for a consolidated quote or invoice.
  • Keys are confidential. Treat license keys like credentials - don't share them across employees or post them internally where they could leak outside the licensed user.
  • Offboarding. If a licensed employee leaves the studio, contact us to reassign that seat to a replacement team member.
  • No redistribution or resale. The product itself can't be redistributed, resold, or sublicensed - see the full Terms of Service for details.
  • Procurement. Need a formal invoice, PO-based purchase, or a signed order form for internal approval? Email us before purchasing and we'll sort it out.
  • Version planning. Because upgrades to a new major version are a separate purchase, budget for that if you plan to stay current with future MasterAssets releases.
  • Check your tier. If your organization's gross revenue is £90,000 or more per year, purchase (or move existing seats to) the Commercial tier - see Indie vs. Commercial pricing above.
